Preliminary shared grammar: this is not generic black cargo or techwear. The repeating structure is
one expressive upper-body surface (detailed black top, original graphic, or tailored black layer)
balanced by distressed/faded black jeans, relaxed slacks, wide cargo, or wide Bermuda denim. Both
tailored and casual branches are valid. Long vertical lines come from blazer/outer drape and long
trousers; other looks deliberately break the vertical line with wide Bermuda proportions. Punk/rock
influence is restrained and expressed through dark tailoring, washed texture, graphics, and footwear
weight rather than dense straps or tactical panels.

For all-black outfits, use a small amount of silver detail to prevent a flat black mass: one or two
localized points such as a belt buckle, necklace, restrained chain, ring, watch, or bag hardware.
Silver must remain subordinate to silhouette and texture. Hardware should be concentrated at the
neck, waist, wrist, or bag—not pasted uniformly across the outfit.

## Preliminary editorial/Pinterest findings

- Subjects are often central but rarely presented as rigid catalog mannequins: weight shifts,
  crossed legs, walking, leaning, seated gestures, or partial side views create movement.
- Camera height is generally around waist-to-chest or natural eye level, with occasional slight low
  angle that makes trouser length and footwear more prominent.
- Framing alternates between neck-down full outfit, deliberately tight torso detail, and contextual
  street crop. The crop is an editorial choice rather than accidental missing anatomy.
- The outfit usually fills most of the vertical frame, while asymmetrical negative space is left in
  streets, walls, interiors, sea/rocks, or architectural lines.
- Backgrounds retain recognizable urban or tactile context instead of seamless studio emptiness.
  Blur is moderate or absent; environment contributes texture without becoming the subject.
- Natural daylight, overcast light, night available light, and warm interior light create believable
  Pinterest/snapshot character. Even lighting, perfectly symmetrical stance, centered isolated model,
  pristine surfaces, and generic shallow-focus architecture are the main catalog signals to avoid.

## Preliminary conflicts with current PRODUCT_SPEC

- The current OTAKU_GRUNGE definition overweights a fixed `FLANNEL_GRUNGE` formula. References show
  flannel as one optional waist/layer device, not a mandatory short overshirt archetype.
- `generic black cargo + short flannel + random keyring` is not supported as the core identity.
- The current three-archetype wording does not sufficiently encode the valid detailed-black-top plus
  distressed black jean/slack branch, the anime/graphic tee plus varied lower branch, or Bermuda denim.
- The existing style descriptions mostly list garments/palettes and do not encode pant break, upper/
  lower volume, top/outer length, footwear weight, accessory placement, or image art direction.
- AMEKAJI needs explicit cuff/hem, tucked-or-compact upper, broad work-trouser volume, and material
  texture rules.
- SOFT_BOYFRIEND/MINIMAL need a clearer distinction between relaxed softness and controlled tailored
  restraint; both may use wide trousers, so garment category alone cannot distinguish them.

### OutfitRecipe policy

- Add silhouette grammar inputs/metadata for top length, outer length, trouser volume, pant break,

  outfit templates.

- Add an all-black rule allowing one or two restrained silver focal points at neck/waist/wrist/bag.
- Preserve abstract/original graphics in generated assets; reference character imagery remains
  grammar evidence only and must never be reproduced.

### ImagePromptBuilder / ArtDirection policy

- Separate outfit description from an explicit art-direction object containing pose, body angle,
  camera height, distance, crop, outfit frame occupancy, negative space, environment, and lighting.
- Prefer natural weight shift, walking/candid, seated editorial, or relaxed asymmetrical stance over
  straight catalog stance.
- Use tactile Seoul street/interior backgrounds with believable available light and controlled
  environmental detail.
- Keep composition-first faceless framing, but vary camera distance and negative space so neck-down
  images do not become ecommerce product grids.
